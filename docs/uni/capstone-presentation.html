Problem

After a lecture, both artifacts fail the student

Goal: keep what was said, not just what was shown — and prove it with numbers.
Solution

Video + slides → grounded conspect + adaptive quiz

Thesis metric — transcript-only coverage: spoken facts absent from slides that survive into the summary. Slides alone score 0% by definition.
Architecture

The pipeline

Two swappable seams — STT and LLM — everything downstream is provider-independent.

E1 · Method

Evaluate at the level of atomic facts, not words

One engine, three directions: coverage, faithfulness and the thesis metric come from the same function.
E1 · Results — lecture 6

Cleaning the transcript moved every metric

baseline — conspect from raw ASR main — conspect from reconstructed transcript
Raw ASR (46 k words) → LLM-reconstructed transcript (8.6 k words, more extractable facts: 526 vs 449). F1 60.7 → 72.6.
E1 · Can we trust the judge?

LLM judge vs 40 hand-labelled pairs

κ = 0.95
39 / 40 agreement (97.5%)
0 false positives → faithfulness is not judge-inflated.
1 miss → coverage numbers are slightly conservative.
The judge is calibrated before its numbers are trusted — and recalibrated after any prompt or model change.
E2 · STT benchmark

Local whisper.cpp beats cloud STT — 7× lower WER

Pipeline default: whisper.cpp — better on accented, terminology-heavy speech, private and $0.
E3 · Question psychometrics

The quiz improves itself

Classical test theory over real attempts — bad questions are retired, archived and replaced automatically (n ≈ 15 caveat stated).
