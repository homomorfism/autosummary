1:00–2:30

Problem & solution

Lectures carry two information streams. Slides persist; speech — the worked examples, caveats, and emphasis — is lost the moment the recording goes unwatched. The service takes lecture.mp4 + slides.pdf and gives the student a URL:

Deliverable 1

Interactive web summary

Fuses both sources; every statement links to its origin — slide N or timestamp T.

Deliverable 2

Adaptive comprehension check

Generated questions, kept only if real response data proves they measure understanding.

Core hypothesis — verified numerically

A meaningful share of lecture content exists only in speech, and can be preserved by multi-source summarization.

2:30–4:00

ML formulation & validation

Speech-to-text

Minimize WER and technical-term error rate (TER). Swappable: whisper.cpp (local) vs Google STT (cloud).

Slide–transcript alignment

Monotone sequence alignment of transcript spans to slides, over timecodes + semantic similarity.

Grounded summarization

Maximize coverage of source facts; every claim must be entailed by a source (faithfulness constraint).

Questions + psychometrics

Estimate difficulty & discrimination from real responses; retire and regenerate failing items.

One entails(claim, source) engine — claim decomposition + NLI — run in both directions gives coverage and faithfulness. WER/TER benchmarked per provider on a golden annotated set. All numbers reproduce from raw data via experiments E1–E3.
